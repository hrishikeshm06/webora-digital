Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Webora Digital ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use CreatorPilot AI, a content-planning and writing workspace.

Current product status: CreatorPilot AI currently runs as a local application on the user's computer. A LinkedIn account connection and LinkedIn publishing integration are not currently active. This policy will be updated before any LinkedIn account connection is enabled.

1. Information the application uses

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Profile details and professional interests that you enter into the application.
  • Post drafts, content plans, calendar entries and performance figures that you enter or create.
  • News-search terms and publicly available article information retrieved from RSS feeds.
  • Application configuration and technical error information needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

Please do not enter passwords, payment-card details, sensitive personal information or information you do not have permission to use.

2. Where information is stored

In the current local version, application data such as profile information, drafts, calendar entries and manually entered analytics is stored in files on the computer where the application is running. You control those files and can delete them from that computer.

Your Gemini API key is configured locally by you. Do not share it or commit it to a public code repository.

3. Third-party services

Google Gemini API: When you request AI assistance, the relevant prompt and context you choose to provide may be sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Google News RSS: When you search for news, your search terms are used to retrieve publicly available headlines and article links through Google News RSS.

We do not sell your personal information. The application does not intentionally send your local drafts or profile data to third parties except where needed for a feature you invoke, such as an AI generation request.

4. LinkedIn

The current version described by this policy does not connect to LinkedIn, access LinkedIn member data, or publish content to LinkedIn.

If a LinkedIn integration is introduced in a future version, we will update this policy to explain the permissions requested, information accessed, token handling, retention, deletion and how to disconnect the integration before enabling that functionality.

5. Data retention and deletion

In the current local version, you generally control retention by managing or deleting the application's local data files. Information sent to Google through an API request may be subject to Google's applicable data-retention terms and settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps in the design and operation of the application to protect information. However, no software or method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your computer, API keys and any local backups.

7. Children's privacy

CreatorPilot AI is intended for professional use and is not directed to children. Do not use the application to submit information about children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application changes or when legal or operational requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
